# Screenshot

Snip, then paste it wherever you want.

That's it? That's it!

<details>
<summary>Details (there aren't many)</summary>

- Default hotkey `Alt+A`; right-click the button to change it.
- Drag a region, or click two corners; right-click or `Esc` to cancel.
- Multi-monitor friendly: snip on one screen, paste on another.
- Requires Windows 10/11 + DSH ≥ 0.2.0.
- Screenshots go straight to your clipboard — nothing leaves your machine.

</details>
